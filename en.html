01 / One question

The camera makes
a game guess.

Ask a question and pass the phone to the participant. Camera only, microphone off. Every new question starts fresh preparation.

  1. 01

    Choose a question

    Pick a light prompt or write your own. The question text is not part of a saved result record.

  2. 02

    Prepare the camera

    Participants agree before the camera starts; preparation takes 45 seconds. The answer then needs about 15 seconds of suitable data; answer capture is limited to 30 seconds.

  3. 03

    See the guess

    The phone may believe, suspect a lie, remain undecided or fail to get a result. Readings are experimental and may be unavailable.

A game guess based on reactions. It does not establish a lie or explain what caused a reaction.

02 / Game with friends

A good story.
Not always a true one.

Pass the phone around.
Look each other in the eye.
A little doubt is part of the game.

  1. 01

    Draw your card

    “Be honest” or “Make it up”. Only the person answering knows their secret role.

  2. 02

    Tell your story

    Answer out loud. Tell the truth or a convincing tale, as your card tells you to.

  3. 03

    Let everyone guess

    In Game with friends, participants receive private roles, answer questions, vote and score points.

  4. 04

    Reveal your card

    Fooled the group or the phone? Collect your points and pass the phone to the next player.

Before the first round

A few
questions.

Where can I download it?

Availability depends on the store. RuStore: version 1.0 is published. Google Play: tester recruitment is ongoing.

How many people can play?

Two to eight, on one phone. Everyone must be 18 or older and agree to take part.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 9 and newer, with a front or rear camera. Even lighting and a steady phone help produce more stable signals.

Can I turn off the microphone?

Yes. The primary Check an answer path has the microphone off. In the separate Observation mode, turn off Voice analysis in Settings; that does not change other paths.

What is kept after a question or observation?

The shared summary-saving preference is off by default. If enabled, each Check an answer result still requires a separate Save action. Game with friends and Observation are separate modes. The record keeps the format and rule versions, time and duration, outcome, any no-result reason and the names of participating factors — without the question text, name or sensor values. An Observation summary also requires a separate Save action.

Why does the phone sometimes pass?

Lighting, noise, movement or a face outside the frame can make the data unreliable. When this happens, the app says there isn’t enough data.
