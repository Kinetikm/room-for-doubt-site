03 / One more player

Your friends bought it.
Did the phone?

The phone plays “lie detector”: it notices changes in pulse, voice and movement, then tries to guess your secret card. A convincing story is only half the fun. See if you can fool the phone, too.

PulseVoiceMovementPupilFacial expression demo

The app does not detect real lies. Its bet is part of the game: the phone can be wrong or pass. Signals are experimental and may be unavailable.

And without the game?

Try Live mode

For two people, with the consent of the person on camera. A 45-second personal baseline, then up to five minutes observing changes. No conclusions about emotions or health.

Before the first round

A few
questions.

Where can I download it?

We’re preparing for release on RuStore and Google Play. Download links will appear here when the app is available. It will be free, with no ads.

How many people can play?

Two to eight, on one phone. Everyone must be 18 or older and agree to take part.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 9 and newer, with a front or rear camera. Even lighting and a steady phone help produce more stable signals.

Can I turn off the microphone?

Yes, in Live mode: turn off Voice analysis in Settings. Voice will be marked as off and left out of the summary.

What is kept after a Live session?

By default, the summary disappears after the session. To keep it, first enable saving in Settings, then tap Save. Only summary metrics are saved locally, without video or audio.

Why does the phone sometimes pass?

Lighting, noise, movement or a face outside the frame can make the data unreliable. When this happens, the app says there isn’t enough data.
